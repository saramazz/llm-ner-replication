4. No Corrections: Retain all typographical errors, unusual formatting, and punctuation as they appear in the source text.

Annotation Guidelines:

1.Identifying Disorder Mentions

2. Use of Synonyms
Annotate terms that are reasonable synonyms of a disorder, even if the exact phrase differs or if they are disjoint.
Example:
Input: The patient reported left-sided facial droop.
Output: The patient reported left-sided [[facial droop]].
Input: His face was weak.
Output: His [[face ... weak]].

3. Explicit Mentions Only
Only annotate disorders explicitly stated in the text.

Only annotate the most specific disorder mentioned in the text.

Input: The patient has a history of stroke.
